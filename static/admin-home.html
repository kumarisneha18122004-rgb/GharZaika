1. Normal Food Order Kaise Karein?

Roz ke khane, snacks ya individual food items ke liye Food Ordering option choose karein.

  1. Open Food Dashboard par click karein.
  2. Available food menu, photos aur prices dekhein.
  3. Apni pasand ke items cart mein add karein.
  4. Quantity aur total amount check karein.
  5. Naam, mobile number aur required details bharein.
  6. Website par available payment option choose karein.
  7. Order place karein aur order ID/status note karein.
  8. Order Ready hone par pickup location se food collect karein.
Pickup Only: Is project mein home delivery available nahi hai. Customer khud food le sakta hai ya kisi doosre vyakti ko bhej sakta hai. Pickup ka time aur address order se pehle confirm karein.

2. Bulk Food / Catering Order Kaise Karein?

Birthday party, family get-together, pooja, kitty party aur chhote office events ke liye Catering & Events choose karein.

  1. Open Catering Dashboard par click karein.
  2. Available menu aur catering packages dekhein.
  3. Apni pasand ki dishes select karein, agar menu selection available ho.
  4. Guests ki sankhya, event type, date aur location bharein.
  5. Estimated total amount aur booking details check karein.
  6. Booking request submit karein.
  7. 25% advance payment ke instructions follow karein, jab payment feature enabled ho.
  8. Payment successful verify hone ke baad booking Confirmed hogi.
  9. Booking ID aur status save karein aur pickup/event arrangements confirm karein.
Payment Note: Booking request submit karna aur booking confirm hona alag steps hain. Payment pending hone par booking ko paid ya confirmed nahi maana jayega. Final price menu, guest count aur agreed services par depend karega.
